The Micro-Fit 3.0 product specification identifies series 43025 as the receptacle housing and
series 43030 as the female crimp terminal mating with series 43045 right-angle PCB headers.

Selected exact parts:

- 430250800: dual-row receptacle housing, 8 circuits, locking, polarized, 3.00 mm pitch, nylon,
  minus 40 to plus 105 degrees Celsius.
- 430250200: dual-row receptacle housing, 2 circuits, locking, polarized, 3.00 mm pitch, nylon,
  minus 40 to plus 105 degrees Celsius.
- 430300038: female tin-plated phosphor-bronze crimp terminal, 18 AWG or 0.75 square millimetre
  conductor, 1.85 mm maximum insulation diameter, 30 mating cycles.

The official application-tool specification ATS-638280200 binds terminal 43030-0038 to 18 AWG,
0.75 square millimetre wire with 1.60 to 1.85 mm insulation diameter and tool 63828-0200. Housing,
terminal, wire, and crimp process are separate selections; this source does not qualify an assembled
harness by itself.
