# Source: JLCPCB economic-parts catalog snapshot, 2026-07-24

Raw source: [economic-parts-2026-07-24.csv](../../Clippings/jlcpcb/economic-parts-2026-07-24.csv).
[captured::2026-07-24] [provider::[[jlcpcb]]]

An externally maintained CSV snapshot of the JLCPCB economic-parts catalog. It contains 2,004
rows observed at 2026-07-24 06:07:31 UTC, with LCSC code, manufacturer model, package, catalog
classification (`base` or `expand`), availability, price, and PCBA minimum quantity/price.

The catalog is a sourcing snapshot, not a substitute for JLCPCB's live quote: stock and price are
volatile, `expand` does not by itself establish a safe electrical equivalent, and absence from this
file does not establish that JLCPCB cannot supply a part as an extended item.
