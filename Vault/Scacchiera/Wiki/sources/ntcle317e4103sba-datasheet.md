# NTCLE317E4103SBA data sheet

Vishay data sheet for the [[ntcle317e4103sba]] cell-contact thermistor. The immutable source is
[[../../Datasheets/NTCLE317E4103SBA_C3154341.pdf]].

The sensor is a 10 kohm NTC with B25/85 of 3984 K, 0.5 percent beta tolerance, and temperature
accuracy of 0.5 degrees Celsius from 25 to 85 degrees Celsius and 1 degree Celsius over the wider
-55 to 150 degree range. Its 1.6 mm epoxy body terminates in 75 mm PEEK-insulated leads. The data
sheet explicitly lists battery cells and packs as applications and permits tape or adhesive
mounting.

[mpn::NTCLE317E4103SBA] [order_code::C3154341] [r25_ohm::10000]
[b25_85_k::3984]

The hub biases the sensor with 10 kohm. Open and short sensor faults map to the cold and hot cutoff
states respectively. The bead is taped to the supplied pouch cell without puncturing or compressing
it.
