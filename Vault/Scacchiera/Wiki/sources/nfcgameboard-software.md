# Source: NFC Game Board — software page

Raw clipping: [Clippings/nfcgameboard.com/software.md](../../Clippings/nfcgameboard.com/software.md). [captured::2026-07-24] [author::[[ben-bulsink]]]

Short landing page stating the project's core software problem (response speed matching human
manipulation, i.e. under ~0.5 s) and its solution: read a whole row or column of tags in one
operation via [[bitwiseid-method|BitwiseID]]'s orthogonal tag coding, rather than one tag at a
time. Reports the resulting 0.35 s response time holds flat even at 225 tiles on the board. Points
to the full [[bitwiseid-whitepaper|BitwiseID white paper]] (captured and transcribed separately,
see [[bitwiseid-method]]) and to two further pages not captured in this round: `/embedded`
(embedded software) and `/presentation` (Windows presentation software).
