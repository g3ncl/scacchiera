# SWPA5045S4R7MT data sheet

Sunlord SWPA series data sheet for the [[swpa5045s4r7mt]] buck inductor. The immutable source is
[[../../Datasheets/SWPA5045S4R7MT_C7373218.pdf]].

The selected 4.7 uH, 20 percent part has 47 mOhm maximum DC resistance, 5 A saturation current,
3.2 A heat-rating current, and a 5 x 5 x 4.5 mm shielded package. The operating range is -40 to
125 degrees Celsius including self-heating.

[mpn::SWPA5045S4R7MT] [order_code::C7373218] [inductance_uh::4.7]
[saturation_a::5] [thermal_a::3.2]

The part exceeds the [[ap63203wu-7]] peak and RMS current requirements at the hub's 2 A regulator
limit and keeps DC resistance below the regulator data sheet's 100 mOhm recommendation.
