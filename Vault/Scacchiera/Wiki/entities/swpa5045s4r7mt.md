# SWPA5045S4R7MT

Sunlord shielded 4.7 uH power inductor selected for hub L1 beside [[ap63203wu-7]]. See
[[swpa5045s4r7mt-datasheet]].

[mpn::SWPA5045S4R7MT] [order_code::C7373218] [manufacturer::Sunlord]

Substitution requires 2.2 to 10 uH, at least 3.2 A thermal current, at least 5 A saturation current,
less than 100 mOhm DC resistance, and a layout recheck.
