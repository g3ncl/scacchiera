# PN5180

NXP's multiprotocol NFC frontend, the reader IC on the
[hub board](../../../../docs/hardware/hub.md) at designator **U3**. It drives the
[matrix board](../../../../docs/hardware/matrix.md)'s shared 13.56 MHz bus and reads the tags in
the pieces.

[mpn::PN5180A0HN/C3E] [lcsc::C1526287] [board::hub] [designator::U3]
[jlc_library::Extended] [package::QFN-40 6x6 P0.5 EP4.6] [unit_cost_eur::3.80]

Datasheet extract on the clock chain: [[pn5180-crystal-and-clock-requirements]].

## Why this reader

ISO/IEC 15693 is the protocol the [[bitwiseid-method]] whole-line reads are proven on, and
[[ben-bulsink]] measured the PN5180 matching the [[clrc632]]'s inventory timings on the
[[nfc-game-board-project]]. That de-risks both the matrix scan rate and the firmware, which is why
it was chosen over continuing with the CLRC632.

## How the hub uses it

- SPI shared with both displays and the matrix selection registers, with NSS, BUSY and IRQ on MCU
  pins and reset on the [[jlcpcb]]-sourced TCA9535 expander.
- TX1 drives the single-ended matrix bus through a 470 nH / 220 pF EMC low-pass and a 68 pF series
  match with a DNP trim. TX2 carries the mirrored EMC filter into ground so the push-pull driver
  stays balanced.
- RX taps the bus through 100 pF and 1 k into RXP, with RXN referenced to VMID.
- Internal 1.8 V LDO tied to AVDD/DVDD; everything else on 3V3. TVDD sits at 3V3, trading field
  strength the 3 mm read budget does not need for one less rail.
- Clock from [[txc-7m27100009]] across CLK1/CLK2 through R27/R28, with C31/C32 to ground.

## Constraints it imposes on the rest of the board

- **27.12 MHz is not negotiable** without changing the clock architecture: it is divided by two to
  make the 13.56 MHz carrier. The ESP32 module's 40 MHz crystal is not a substitute and is not
  brought out to a pin, so the board needs its own crystal. The PLL would accept 8, 12, 16 or
  24 MHz instead, which is the only route to deleting the crystal.
- Table 142 bounds the crystal: 10 pF load, ESR 100 ohm max, ± 100 ppm, 100 uW drive max.
- QFN-40 with a 4.6 mm exposed pad, so it is reflow-only. It is one of the seven Extended lines the
  build plan deliberately leaves with JLCPCB rather than hand-fitting.

## Validation

`hardware/tests/test_sim_hub.py` drives the full 16-cell matrix bus through this TX path and checks
[criteria.yaml](../../../../docs/hardware/criteria.yaml): the selected loop's field peaks at
13.86 MHz at 60 mA of coil current per volt of drive. The 68 pF match value came from that bench.
