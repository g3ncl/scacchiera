# AP22811AW5-7

Diodes Incorporated 2 A power-distribution switch selected for hub U1. It gates the external 5 V
input to the [[pisugar3-plus]] input pad under the hardware temperature window. See
[[ap22811aw5-7-datasheet]].

[mpn::AP22811AW5-7] [order_code::C3001660] [manufacturer::Diodes-Incorporated]

Substitution requires active-high control, at least 2 A continuous current, current and thermal
protection, output discharge, and reverse-current blocking at 5 V.
