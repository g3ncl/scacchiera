# TLV7042

Texas Instruments dual rail-to-rail open-drain comparator selected for hub U10. It implements the
independent hot and cold charge window around the cell thermistor. See [[tlv7042-datasheet]].

[mpn::TLV7042DGKR] [order_code::C2760466] [manufacturer::Texas-Instruments]

Substitution requires two rail-to-rail inputs, open-drain outputs, operation from 5 V, internal
hysteresis, and characterized power-on behavior.
