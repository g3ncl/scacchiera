# TPS25730S

Texas Instruments autonomous sink-only USB-C PD controller selected for the hub's input. The
[[tps25730-datasheet|data sheet]] defines the device and [[pmp23456-reference-design]] demonstrates
it negotiating 5 V/3 A or 9 V/3 A for the selected charger.

[mpn::TPS25730SRSMR] [manufacturer::Texas-Instruments] [package::VQFN-32]

The part replaces passive CC pull-downs as the authority for source capability. Its configuration
strap and external sink path are release-critical V2 and V3 evidence.
