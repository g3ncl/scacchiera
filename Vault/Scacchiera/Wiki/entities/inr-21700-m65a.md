# INR-21700-M65A

Molicel 6.5 Ah high-energy 21700 cell selected as the cell inside the proposed protected chessboard
battery assembly. Its [[inr-21700-m65a-datasheet|manufacturer data sheet]] permits substantially
faster charging than the rejected Adafruit 4400 mAh pack while its 21.7 by 70.2 mm cylinder remains
plausible inside a 50 mm player rail.

[mpn::INR-21700-M65A] [manufacturer::Molicel] [category::battery-cell]

This entity does not represent a finished pack. Binding remains incomplete until a qualified pack
assembler, protection circuit, thermistor, insulation, lead gauge, and locking connector are exact.
