# INR-21700-M65A

Molicel 6.5 Ah high-energy 21700 cell selected as the candidate cell inside the proposed protected
chessboard battery assembly. Its [[inr-21700-m65a-datasheet|manufacturer data sheet]] rates 26 A
continuous discharge while its 21.7 by 71.0 mm conservative envelope fits lengthwise inside a 50
mm player rail.

[mpn::INR-21700-M65A] [manufacturer::Molicel] [category::battery-cell]

This entity does not represent a finished pack. Binding remains incomplete until a qualified pack
assembler, protection circuit, thermistor, insulation, lead gauge, and locking connector are exact.
NKON reported the exact flat-top cell out of stock on 2026-07-30. The pack protection must directly
disconnect both charge and discharge on overtemperature; the hub's separate charge interlock is
redundant protection, not a substitute.
