# SW6106

Zhuhai iSmartWare single-cell bidirectional fast-charge power-bank controller fitted to the
[[rbs18634]] candidate test article. The [[sw6106-datasheet|data sheet]] specifies 4 A charging
above 5 V input, 18 W USB-C PD input and output, simultaneous charge and output, and an NTC input.

[mpn::SW6106] [manufacturer::Zhuhai-iSmartWare] [package::QFN-40]

The chessboard does not bind this IC into its final hub schematic. It is evidence for a low-cost
test article whose module implementation still needs inspection and measurement.
