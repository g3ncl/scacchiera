# CSD25404Q3

Exact fitted component from Texas Instruments, used by power Q1 (CSD25404Q3).

[mpn::CSD25404Q3] [supplier::JLCPCB]
[order_code::C2865523] [category::semiconductor]

The immutable source is summarized in [[csd25404q3-datasheet]]. The complete
library, rating, availability, and model audit is machine checked from
`docs/verification/v1-components.yaml`.

Power Q1 places its drain clip at the external cell positive terminal and its source clip at the protected `BAT_RAW` node. Its body diode bootstraps only correct polarity, after which [[tlv7021dckr]] pulls the gate low. The hot 4.422 A RMS loss bound is 0.331 W. The routed board uses TI's DQG land pattern and a clearance rule scoped only to Q1's 0.15 mm manufacturer pad gaps.
