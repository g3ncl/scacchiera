# AP63203WU-7

Diodes Incorporated 2 A fixed 3.3 V synchronous buck selected for hub U2. It converts the
commercial subsystem's regulated 5 V output to the logic rail. See [[ap63203wu-7-datasheet]].

[mpn::AP63203WU-7] [order_code::C780769] [manufacturer::Diodes-Incorporated]

Substitution requires a fixed 3.3 V synchronous buck rated for at least 2 A from 5 V, compatible
with the filed support parts and validated layout.
