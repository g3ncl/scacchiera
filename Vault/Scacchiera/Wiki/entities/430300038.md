# 430300038

Molex tin-plated female Micro-Fit 3.0 terminal selected for the [[430250800]] and [[430250200]]
housings. It accepts 18 AWG or 0.75 square millimetre wire. The exact wire and qualified crimp or
pre-crimped lead remain open. Evidence is summarized in [[micro-fit-3-0-mating-evidence]].

**Rating is catalog evidence, 2026-08-01.** This terminal carries the cell link, the
highest-current path in the product: 4.442 A RMS through one contact pair. The filed PS-43045 revision M1
tops out at 20 AWG at 5 A and has no 18 AWG row; distributors list this variant at **8.5 A**,
which the design accepts as catalog evidence pending a current Molex revision. See
[[micro-fit-current-rating]]. The harness passes either way: 48 percent margin at 8.5 A, 11
percent at 5.0 A.
