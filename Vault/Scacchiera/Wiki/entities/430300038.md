# 430300038

Molex tin-plated female Micro-Fit 3.0 terminal selected for the [[430250800]] and [[430250200]]
housings. It accepts 18 AWG or 0.75 square millimetre wire. The exact wire and qualified crimp or
pre-crimped lead remain open. Evidence is summarized in [[micro-fit-3-0-mating-evidence]].

**Superseded for the cell link, 2026-08-01.** The design moved to the documented 20 AWG at 5.0 A
row of PS-43045, so the cell harness now uses the 20 to 24 AWG tin terminal 43030-0007 and this
18 AWG part is no longer fitted there. It was displaced because its own rating is unresolved: The filed PS-43045 revision M1 tops out at 20 AWG at
5 A and its bundled terminal drawing states 5.0 A maximum; distributors list this 18 AWG variant
at 8.5 A, which is a catalog figure rather than a datasheet one. See
[[micro-fit-current-rating]]. At 8.5 A the harness has 31 percent margin; at 5.0 A it is 17
percent over.
