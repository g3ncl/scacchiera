# TXC 7M27100009

The 27.12 MHz crystal clocking the [[pn5180]] on the
[hub board](../../../../docs/hardware/hub.md), at designator **Y1**.

[mpn::7M27100009] [lcsc::C90919] [board::hub] [designator::Y1]
[jlc_library::Extended] [package::SMD3225-4P] [frequency_mhz::27.12]
[unit_cost_eur::0.17]

Datasheet values: [[txc-7m27100009-datasheet]]. The requirement it serves:
[[pn5180-crystal-and-clock-requirements]].

## Why this part

It replaced an Epson EXS00A-CS01188 that JLCPCB reported six pieces short, which together with the
[[esp32-c6-mini-1u]] shortfall was blocking the hub assembly order. Selection was driven by the
reader's Table 142 rather than by price, and this part clears every line of it:

| Requirement | PN5180 limit | This part |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Load capacitance | 10 pF typ | 10 pF |
| ESR | 50 typ, 100 max | 60 ohm |
| Frequency tolerance | ± 100 ppm | ± 10 ppm |
| Stability over temperature | (inside ± 100 ppm) | ± 15 ppm |
| Drive level | 100 uW max | 100 uW typ |

Stock at capture was 1,905 against a five-board order, and it is available for Economic PCBA.

## What it changed on the board

The package went from 2016 (2.0 x 1.6 mm) to **3225** (3.2 x 2.5 mm), which needed a footprint
change but is otherwise welcome: the larger pads are reworkable by hand if the part ever has to come
off, where the 2016's four underside pads were not.

More importantly it fixed the load capacitors. C31 and C32 went from 10 pF to **15 pF**. Two equal
capacitors present `CL = C/2 + Cstray` to the crystal, so with 2 to 4 pF of trace and CLK-pin stray:

- 15 pF presents about 10.5 pF against the required 10 pF, roughly 10 ppm of pull;
- the previous 10 pF presented about 8 pF, roughly 41 ppm of pull.

The old pair was inside the ± 100 ppm budget but spent most of it on load error before the crystal's
own tolerance was counted. 15 pF is JLCPCB Basic (C1548, `0402CG150J500NT`), so the correction cost
nothing in feeder fees.

## Open items

- Drive level sits **at** the reader's 100 uW ceiling rather than below it, the one parameter with
  no margin. The series resistors R27/R28 are the knob if the assembled board needs the drive
  reduced; the datasheet notes their value depends on the crystal chosen.
- Stray capacitance is estimated at 2 to 4 pF, not measured. If the assembled board's carrier sits
  off frequency, C31/C32 are the first thing to trim.
