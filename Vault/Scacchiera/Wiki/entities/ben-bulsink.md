# Ben Bulsink

[role::Author and builder] of the [[nfc-game-board-project|NFC Game Board]] project (nfcgameboard.com) and
of the [[bitwiseid-method|BitwiseID]] white paper. Contact given on the site as
`benbulsink@outlook.com`.

Builds and documents RFID-sensed physical game boards (a Scrabble board, in the tested
prototype) as a hobby/open project, publishing schematics (sPlan), PCB layouts (Sprint
Layout), firmware notes, and a formal write-up of the BitwiseID detection method. States the
BitwiseID white paper is third-party GPLv3 free documentation, © 2016-2025.

As of the [[nfcgameboard-home|home page]] capture (2026-07-24), an active redesign is underway
to move the reader from the [[clrc632|CLRC632]] to the NXP PN5180 multi-protocol reader,
reusing community Github libraries and matching the CLRC632's measured timing (9 ms Inventory,
12 ms ReadSingleBlock).
