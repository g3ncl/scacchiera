# BQ25638

Texas Instruments 5 A single-cell switching charger and NVDC power-path controller selected to
replace the linear MCP73871 on the hub. The [[bq25638-datasheet|data sheet]] defines its limits and
[[pmp23456-reference-design]] supplies measured application evidence.

[mpn::BQ25638YBGR] [manufacturer::Texas-Instruments] [package::DSBGA-30]

It is a factory-placement part. V1 must bind current supply and audit the 30-ball footprint before
the selection becomes complete.
